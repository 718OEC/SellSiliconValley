History Rhymes: The AI Parallel

Today's AI boom feels eerily similar to the Dot-Com surge of 1999/2000. Just like then, we are seeing immense wealth creation. But even when the Dot-Com bubble burst in 2001, home prices merely paused and didn't crash until the unrelated subprime bubble years later.

The Lesson: Market crashes usually aren't a good time to buy simply because it's in a moment of economic uncertainty.

2026 Investor Outlook

Financing

The Rate Pivot

Investment property is highly sensitive to debt cost. A predicted rate cut would instantly reverse the massive discounts we see today.

Yield

4.25% Cap Rate

Fourplexes are the "Yield Kings" of 2026. This cap rate expansion is purely a function of price correction, not a loss of rental income.

Opportunity

The Contrarian Play

While everyone chases Single Family homes at all-time highs, the smart money is quietly absorbing undervalued 2-4 unit inventory.

2026 Strategy

Buy the "4"

Focus exclusively on Fourplex assets. The market has over-corrected on these units. The median price is incredibly favorable ($1.8M vs $2.0M) and they're generally even less than the median in San Jose.

Wait for Rates

Do not sell your 2-4 unit property in Q1. Buyers are constrained by rates. Wait for the Fed pivot in Q2/Q3 to allow buyer purchasing power to recover before listing.
