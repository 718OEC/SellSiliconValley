Cap Rate Trends

Yield is King. With the price drop, Fourplex Cap Rates have expanded to 4.25%, the highest median yield in the residential sector.

Cap rate: Your annual return percentage if you buy with cash.
(Annual net operating income divided by the purchase price. The higher the better.)

GRM Trends

Gross Rent Multiplier. The "Double Value" event: Duplex income rose 8% ($60k → $64.8k) while prices fell 7%, compressing GRM significantly.

GRM: The number of years for rent to pay off the price.
(The purchase price divided by gross annual rental income.)

2026 Investor Outlook

Financing

The Rate Pivot

Investment property is sensitive to debt cost. A predicted Q2 rate cut would instantly reverse the 13.5% discount we see today.

Yield

4.25% Cap Rate

Fourplexes are the "Yield Kings" of 2026. This cap rate expansion is purely a function of price correction, not income loss.

Opportunity

The Contrarian Play

While everyone chases Single Family homes at all-time highs, the smart money is quietly absorbing undervalued 2-4 unit inventory.

Market Cycles

2008
The Great Financial Crisis

Cap rates compressed to near zero before the crash. Prices bottomed in 2009.

2015
The Million Dollar Norm

Single Family and Multi-Family assets permanently crossed the 7-figure mark.

2021
Peak Appreciation

Cheap money drove all asset classes (Houses, Duplexes, Triplexes) to move in lockstep.

2025
The Great Divergence

The correlation broke. Houses hit new highs ($2.0M+), while Fourplexes dropped 13.5% to $1.8M.

This divergence suggests the "Investment Premium" has vanished, making Multi-family cheaper than Single Family for the first time in years.

2026 Strategy

Buy the "4"

Focus exclusively on Fourplex assets. The market has over-corrected on these units. The median price is favorable ($1.8M vs $2.0M) and they're generally even less than the median in San Jose.

Wait for Rates

Do not sell your 2-4 unit property in Q1. Buyers are constrained by rates. Wait for the Fed pivot in Q2/Q3 to allow buyer purchasing power to recover before listing.
