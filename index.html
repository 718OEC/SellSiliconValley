About

For students, by students: an open-source campus initiative

This project was started as a way to compile the active clubs at both WVC and MC. It quickly branched out into the other pages into this site, where the purpose is to provide accessible resources to all students for their time at CC. My goal is for this project to be maintained by students in perpetuity so it serves students for perpetuity. This will always be a work in progress, excuse any hiccups please.
